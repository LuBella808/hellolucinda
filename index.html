Meet Lucinda Rae

Brand Strategist. Visibility Architect. Author. Substack Expert.

For 22+ years, Lucinda Rae has been doing one thing: helping spiritual women entrepreneurs get out of their own way and into the full, luminous expression of their calling. As a web designer, brand photographer, graphic artist, and visibility strategist, she has built over 300 brands - and knows better than anyone that the most powerful thing a woman can do for her business is stop hiding the depths of what she carries.

She is the multi-award-winning author of Holy Hot Visibility™ - a proprietary framework built on the 7 Visibility Gateways, an embodied map for understanding where energy and belief get stuck, and how co-creating with the Divine liberates them into full, confident expression. She is also a spiritual artist, illustrator, and intuitive mentor with a strong connection to the angelic realm who can see into your higher potential with uncanny clarity - and translate that vision into the psychology of a brand that looks and feels exactly like you.

Lucinda built her own solid, profitable 6-figure business from scratch and teaches the same alchemy of proven strategies, spiritually grounded mindset, and energetic methodology that creates a real paradigm shift in your business and life. She fast-tracks the learning she wishes she had known sooner - and when she got on Substack, she made $7,200 in her first ten days, not from a big launch, but from showing up as exactly who she is. Now she builds yours.

She sees you in your fullest potential. She holds you to the sacred fire of your divine destiny while teaching you the structures and systems you need for wild business success - so you can rise with the sun and meet your day with radiant joy and pleasure.
