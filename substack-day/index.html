Questions

Good to know

Do I need to be available all day?+

No. We do the deep thinking together in our strategy meeting beforehand. On your build day I work on my own, and we connect once at the halfway point so you can see progress and share feedback.

What if I don't have a Substack yet?+

That's perfectly fine. We can set one up together before your day so everything is ready for me to build.

What do I need to prepare?+

You'll fill out a short intake questionnaire and bring any existing articles, photos, or brand pieces you'd like included. If you're starting fresh, I'll guide you through it.

Will it really sound like me?+

Yes. Everything I write comes from our deep-dive conversation and your intake, and you'll review every page so you can request adjustments before your reveal.

What happens after my day?+

You'll have a finished publication and a clear content system to keep publishing with ease. If you'd like ongoing strategy or support, I'll share the ways we can keep working together.
