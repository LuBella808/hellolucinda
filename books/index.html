Psalm 23:
You Are With Me

Mom's Choice Award Moonbeam Children's Book Award Purple Dragonfly Award Winner Reader's Favorite ★★★★★

Psalm 23 is one of the most beloved songs of trust in the Bible. The simple, profound and beautiful verses have the power to comfort and bring peace to many. When we go through troubled times, this psalm helps us remember God is always present.

Lucinda Rae's illustrations invite the reader on a visual journey into the wilderness with a young shepherdess, accompanied by her trusted dog and a lamb, reminding us that we are being guided by the ultimate shepherd, God. The paintings evoke an emotional response including contentment and confidence, inviting us into a personal relationship with the Creator. Each verse is painted individually to convey each step on the journey of this psalm. The ancient wisdom within this song is relevant today and continues to benefit readers young and old.

Poetry & Prayer

Love Notes with Eternity:
Flashes of Illumination

And Other Poetic Prayerful Tributes to the Beloved Divine

These poems, prayers, and flashes of illumination are from a one or two yearlong journey when Lucinda's first son moved from home and out-of-state much earlier than she had ever imagined, causing deep grief to her mother's heart. This caused her to turn inward and fervently seek the Divine for understanding, loving wisdom, and healing.

From this space, she devoted herself to daily morning and evening prayers to help get through the difficult times. She learned to transmute the various pains we experience while being human, and the adversities life can bring — and discovered that suffering could be turned into something beautiful and breathable. Living in the realm of this period of transformation, she came to know Divinity as the quiet whispers that seed her prayers to offer profound insights and wisdom for the soul sojourn.

In the human longing for love, we can become deeply intimate with God, the most Beloved Divine. May these love notes with eternity inspire you on your journey with a sense of comfort and a love that never fades, and Light that endures beyond the setting sun.
